Modifications made in this repository:

  - Namespace renamed from Dynamitey / Dynamitey.Internal.Optimization to
    FSharp.Interop.Dynamic.BridgeSupport / .Internal.
  - Trimmed to the members FSharp.Interop.Dynamic calls: the invoke, get,
    set, index, chain, convert, binary-operator, add/subtract-assign and
    is-event paths. Constructors, unary operators, Curry, CoerceConvert,
    CoerceToDelegate, InvokeSetAll, Linq, GetMemberNames, FastDynamicInvoke,
    the COM / ImpromptuInterface / TypeDescriptor late-bound hooks,
    DynamicObjects, Builder, Return<T>/ThisFunc delegate helpers, PartialApply,
    Tupler, FluentRegex and BareBonesList are not included.
  - InvokeGetChain / InvokeSetChain read the Regex groups directly instead of
    going through FluentRegex and CoerceConvert.
  - Invocations with more than 14 arguments build their delegate type with
    Expression.GetDelegateType instead of ImpromptuInterface's emitter.
  - The static factory fields (InvokeContext.CreateStatic, InvokeArg.Create,
    InvokeMemberName.Create, ...) are plain lambdas. Upstream built them with
    Return<T>.Arguments, and Return<T> is not vendored.
  - Util is internal; unused members removed.
  - Each file carries a header line naming its upstream source.
